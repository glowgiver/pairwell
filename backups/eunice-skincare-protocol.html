The golden rules

⚠ High Focus Shot (MFU) boundaries

Maximum once per week. Completely avoid the eyeballs, eyelids, jawbone edge, lips and the centre of the neck (thyroid area). Target only the jowls, lower face sag and the sides of the neck.

⚠ The no-mix actives rule

Never use the Exosome Shot or the Booster Pro Air Shot on the same night as 0.2% Retinol, and never on a High Focus Shot night.

The conductivity rule

Use a thick layer of PDRN Booster Gel for the High Focus Shot, or PDRN Pink Peptide Serum for the Booster Pro. Oils (Freiöl, Rose Hip Oil) insulate — always apply them after device use, never before.

The 'Kalk' hard-water shield

Hamburg tap water dulls skin and stretches pores. Use the Foreo in the evening to strip mineral buildup mechanically, and always apply The Ordinary HA 2% + B5 while the skin is still damp.
